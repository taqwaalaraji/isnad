# Guide for Journalists

You send content to Isnad, read what it found, fix the transcript if needed, and send the check to an editor. Editors make the decisions ([guide-editor.md](guide-editor.md)).

## Sign in

Open the link in your invitation email, or go to the sign-in page and choose a one-time link by email or your password («الدخول بكلمة المرور»). If you belong to several newsrooms, switch from the newsroom card at the top of the sidebar.

## Start a check

From **Home** («الرئيسية») drop a file on the drop zone, or choose **New check** («تحقق جديد»).

1. Choose the input:
   - **File** («ملف»): video, audio, PDF, Word or text, up to 500 MB.
   - **Link** («رابط»): an article, press release or official page. Video-platform links (YouTube, TikTok, X, Instagram) aren't supported: download the clip and upload it.
   - **Text** («نص»): paste the story or statement.
2. Fill in the details:
   - **Check title** («عنوان التحقق») — shown in lists and in the isnad note.
   - **Recorded or published on** («تاريخ التسجيل أو النشر») — needed to read "this year" or "last quarter" correctly.
   - **Desk** («القسم»), **Speakers** («المتحدثون»), **Dialect** («اللهجة») for audio and video.
   - **Who can see this check** («من يرى هذا التحقق»): only you, your desk, or the whole newsroom.
3. Press **Start check** («ابدأ التحقق»).

If your newsroom has reached its monthly limit, Isnad says so and doesn't create the check.

## While it processes

The check page shows the stages: preparing the clip, transcribing, finding the numbers, matching and verifying. Numbers appear as soon as each one is checked. You can leave the page; you'll get a "check ready" alert («اكتمل التحقق»).

To stop it, press **Cancel check** («إلغاء التحقق»). If it fails, the page says why (for example, no readable text, or the link couldn't be opened) and offers **Try again**.

Clips that carry their own caption track are read from the captions. Others are transcribed automatically, with speakers and word timings.

## Read the results

The check page has:

- **Numbers** («الأرقام»): a card per number with its verdict, its place (time or paragraph) and the chain state. **Needs a decision only** («بحاجة لقرار فقط») hides decided numbers. Use `j` and `k` to move between numbers.
- **Transcript** («التفريغ») for audio and video, or **Text** («النص») for documents.
- **Activity** («النشاط»): who did what.
- The **stage**: the video or audio player with captions and coloured markers on the timeline, or the document with the numbers highlighted. Click a marker or a number to jump to it. **Follow playback** («تتبّع التشغيل») keeps the current number in view; **Pause at each issue** («توقف عند كل ملاحظة») stops at every number that differs or has a different context.
- The **evidence panel** for the selected number: the official document and the exact quote, what the claim says vs what the document says, the suggested wording, the automatic verifier's note, earlier figures, and comments.

Numbers that weren't checked (opinions, forecasts) are listed under **Not checked** («لم يُتحقق منها») with the reason.

A number marked **Not searched yet** («لم يُبحث بعد») wasn't in the registry and wasn't among the numbers Isnad searched automatically. Press **Search official sources** («ابحث في المصادر الرسمية») to search for it.

## Fix the transcript

In the **Transcript** tab, hover a line and press the edit icon. Correct the words and press **Save and re-check** («حفظ وإعادة التحقق»). Only the numbers in that line are checked again.

## Send for review

Press **Send for review** («إرسال للمراجعة»), choose an editor or **Any editor on the desk** («أي محرر في القسم»), and send. The editor gets an alert.

## Other actions

- **Comment** on any number in the evidence panel.
- **Re-check against today's registry** («إعادة التحقق بسجل اليوم») from the **⋯** menu, when official figures may have changed.
- **Export** («تصدير»): copy the **isnad note** («ملاحظة الإسناد», approved numbers only) or download **subtitles (SRT)** for audio and video.
- **Archive** («أرشفة») your own checks.

## Use the registry

**Isnad Registry** («سجل الإسناد») lets you search indicators, entities, figures and documents, open an indicator's history and chart, and **Follow** («متابعة») indicators and entities to get alerts on new figures and releases. **Ask Isnad** («اسأل إسناد») answers questions from the registry only, with evidence for every number.

## Briefs

**Briefs** («الإحاطات») prepare you for an interview: choose the guest's entity, an optional topic, guest name and interview date, and Isnad writes a sheet with the entity's latest official figures, recent revisions, what it said before in your newsroom's checks, and suggested questions. Every number links to its evidence. Use **Print** («طباعة») or copy the link for colleagues.

## Settings

In **Settings** («الإعدادات») set your name, interface language, theme and password; choose which alerts reach you on Telegram; and link your Telegram account by scanning the code or pressing **Open Telegram** («فتح تيليجرام»). The link works once, for ten minutes.
